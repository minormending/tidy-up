Grown-up settings

Jobs

Photograph the real bin or shelf, and record the instruction in your own voice. Both stay on this device — nothing is uploaded.

How much help

Step this down as they need it less. A system with no exit becomes permanent scaffolding.

Timer and sound

Set this from what they can actually sustain, not from what sounds reasonable. A timer they keep failing is training failure.

Stars

Nothing in the app ever takes a star away. Clearing is a grown-up action, on purpose.

Getting back here

Press and hold the round button in the top corner of the screen for about a second and a half. It fills up while you hold it. A quick tap does nothing, which is what keeps a five-year-old out.

When everything is finished the app settles into a quiet summary of the day. Press and hold anywhere on that screen to go back to the pictures.

Backup and moving

A backup file carries everything — jobs, photos, recordings, stars. Keep one somewhere safe: clearing the browser’s site data erases the setup and there is no copy anywhere else.

A setup code moves the job list to another device by camera. Names, timers, and support level only — a QR code holds about 2.9KB and one photo is fifty times that.

Two devices at once

Keeps the day the same on both: a job finished on the tablet shows as done on your phone a moment later. Stars, today’s ticks and the run log travel.

Jobs, photos and recordings do not. Those move with a backup file or the setup code above — a photo of your child’s room and your recorded voice stay on the device they were made on.

Type the code shown on the other device. Connecting merges the two, and nothing is ever lowered, so no star or tick is lost either way.
