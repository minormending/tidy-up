Grown-up settings

Jobs

Photograph the real bin or shelf, and record the instruction in your own voice. Both stay on this device — nothing is uploaded.

How much help

Step this down as he needs it less. A system with no exit becomes permanent scaffolding.

Timer and sound

Set this from what he can actually sustain, not from what sounds reasonable. A timer he keeps failing is training failure.

Stars

Nothing in the app ever takes a star away. Clearing is a grown-up action, on purpose.

Backup and moving

A backup file carries everything — jobs, photos, recordings, stars. Keep one somewhere safe: clearing the browser’s site data erases the setup and there is no copy anywhere else.

A setup code moves the job list to another device by camera. Names, timers, and support level only — a QR code holds about 2.9KB and one photo is fifty times that.
